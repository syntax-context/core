The band syntax

It breaks up a band of individual practitioners, each doing their own thing, who show and publish together in episodes. It has 7 element types and 21 elements, and a band described in it is one choice of elements from each type.

Read from Bairang's own documents on 13 November 2026 (his words that day: "bairang is a kind of thing which has syntax - it is example"). Bairang is the band described in it.

Instances

No tool is built on it yet.

Element types and elements

curator holds one essential

The person who starts the band, fixes each episode's question and writes every invitation.

one of the people
the curator also makes a work for the episode, unsigned like the rest
outside the band
the curator invites and shows, and makes no work

people holds several essential

Individual practitioners, each doing their own thing. They practise something other than art.

research
someone whose practice is finding things out
writing
someone whose practice is writing
sound
someone who makes sound
making
someone who makes things with their hands or with machines
teaching
someone whose practice is teaching
code
someone who writes code

question holds one essential

The provocation each invitation carries for an episode. Each person makes one work in answer to it.

way in holds several essential

How a person comes into an episode. There is no open call.

invitation
the curator writes to the person and asks for one work in answer to the question
proposing yourself
a person or a space proposes itself, to be considered for being invited to pitch
a pitch under a token
the invited person pitches under a token, with no name, so the pitch is read without knowing whose it is

space holds one essential

Where an episode is shown. The curator writes to spaces and asks what they could offer, and invites them to apply or pitch.

library
a library
bookshop
a bookshop
school
a school
makerspace
a makerspace
print shop
a print shop
research centre
a research centre

signing holds one essential

Whether a work carries its maker's name.

unsigned
no work carries its maker's name; a ledger keeps which token is whose, closed for ten years, and each maker can claim their work when they choose, or never
signed
each work carries its maker's name

episode holds several essential

One round of the band: one question, its works, shown together.

shown in a space
the works are shown together, for real, in the space
published online
the works are published together online

Left out

Artists and art spaces: the people of a band in this syntax practise something other than art, and its spaces are not art spaces. It also leaves out a list of who made which work, an open call, and money.
